THE VERIFICATION HABIT

Don't stop at the headline.

01 / FIND THE ORIGINAL

Who first published the claim? Can you find the complete article, report, or official statement?

02 / CHECK THE CONTEXT

Check the date, location, numbers, and what happened before and after.

03 / COMPARE SOURCES

Look for independent reporting and inspect the original evidence.

04 / DON'T RUSH

Viral does not mean verified. If you cannot verify it, don't present it as established fact.

05 / THINK BEFORE SHARING

A few seconds of verification can stop misinformation from spreading further.
